# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers and technical leads at AI, robotics, and software companies who open the portfolio to decide whether to move Ahmad forward to interview.

Research collaborators (professors, lab directors, co-authors) evaluating rigor, methodology, and fit for serious technical collaboration.

ML practitioners and engineers who arrive through Field Notes and evaluate credibility through the quality of arguments, systems thinking, and technical specificity.

## Product Purpose

This product is Ahmad Firas Azfar's portfolio and technical publishing platform. It is meant to create an immersive journey through his life and work so visitors quickly understand who he is, how he thinks, and what he can build.

Success means visitors reach project/research depth, see specific outcomes and evidence, and leave with a concrete understanding of Ahmad's engineering and research capability.

## Positioning

An immersive, evidence-backed journey that helps visitors understand Ahmad through both shipped work and thought process, rather than through static resume-style presentation.

## Operating Context

Primary use is desktop/laptop review, often in short evaluation windows for hiring decisions.

Visitors navigate across narrative portfolio sections and long-form Field Notes content, shifting between skim and deep-reading behavior.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Current site structure and sections are intentional and must be preserved unless Ahmad explicitly requests a change.

Portfolio content and Field Notes function as two connected paths: proof of execution and proof of thinking.

Product must remain web-first and production-deployable on the current Next.js stack.

## Brand Commitments

Name and identity are Ahmad Firas Azfar and his personal domain portfolio presence.

Voice commitment: precise, deliberate, credible, and opinionated without hype.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing project/result claims and narrative content are already present in repository content modules and Field Notes sources.

Evidence artifacts include case-study and project-specific metrics already referenced in portfolio content and section data.

Known content locations: `src/lib/data/`, `src/content/field-notes/`, and related route surfaces in `src/app/`.

## Product Principles

Credibility is the core product outcome.

The experience should hook visitors and pull them through a meaningful journey of Ahmad's life and work.

Structure and specificity should make technical depth easy to verify, not merely claim.

Content quality and argument quality are first-class product outputs, not secondary marketing material.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline.

Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid relying on color alone for critical meaning.
